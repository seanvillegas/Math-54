### Disclaimer to fellow classmates
Do not blindly copy my answers. Approach quizzes, midterms, homeworks as 10 minute attempts for easy questions, 25 minutes for hard ones. If you cannot come up with the idea yourself, you need to train your hueristics. Look at the solution, and attempt another problem (subject not related). Then come back to that problem, and try it again. Then once you convince yourself of solving the last problem, attempt another one of the same subject. Then you revisit the subject in 2 days, 1 week, 2 weeks ...., and so forth to be prepared for exams and really digest the material. You are only hurting yourself if you just look at solutions and dont understand them.
